The problem

A plan for no one in particular is a plan for nothing.

A stock 12-week program doesn’t know your climb is in 19 weeks. It doesn’t know you’ll carry a 40-pound pack up 4,000 feet in a day, or that you need to taper the week you fly out. So you show up under-prepared, over-trained, or both.

Switchback starts from the one date that matters — your summit — and works backward. Everything else in the app bends to fit it.

How it works

Name the mountain. We build the climb.

Four moves from “someday” to summit-ready.

01

The killer feature

One trip reshapes everything.

This is what makes Switchback different from every other training app. Your objective isn’t a note in a profile — it’s the spine of the whole program.

  • The calendar ends on your trip date, with the final week as a taper so you’re fresh at the trailhead.
  • The rucks ramp their pack weight and elevation toward your objective — building to your real load by the peak weeks.
  • The cardio grows for altitude when your climb tops 12,000 feet, so thin air isn’t a surprise.
  • The lifts swap to match your style — grip and calves for technical, carries and heavy step-ups for expeditions.

Set it once. Every tab quietly adjusts for the rest of the block.
